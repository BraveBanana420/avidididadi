Avidididadi plans and guides your training: strength, running, swimming, cycling, mobility and climbing. The plan adapts to how your sessions feel, the guided workout takes you step by step, and Progress shows how you improve.

<b>Your data stays on your phone</b>: no server, no account, no ads, no tracking. Available in Italian, English and Greek.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li><b>Plan</b>: calendar with an optional typical week; generators for strength, running, swimming, cycling and mobility; progression by duration or intensity; adapts to your feedback and recovery.</li>
<li><b>Today</b>: today's session, your week, energy from sleep, HRV and resting heart rate.</li>
<li><b>Guided workout</b>: timer, rests, beeps and voice; GPS for running, walking and cycling (also with the screen off); routes with turn-by-turn voice directions.</li>
<li><b>Smart trainer</b> (FTMS): ERG mode with the watts of each block; heart rate straps and bike sensors via Bluetooth.</li>
<li><b>Threshold tests</b>: bike FTP (ramp or 20 minutes), running FTP, critical swim speed.</li>
<li><b>Health Connect</b> (read-only): sessions from other apps, heart rate, sleep, HRV, routes.</li>
<li><b>intervals.icu</b> (optional, your API key): activities from your watch and planned sessions sent to your calendar.</li>
<li><b>Maps</b>: MapLibre + OpenFreeMap, GPX import, offline area around a route.</li>
<li><b>Coach</b> (optional): an AI assistant with your own API key, Ollama on your PC, or your assistant's app with a link code.</li>
<li><b>More</b>: exercise catalog with animations, muscle map, home screen widget, shareable images, backup and restore to a file.</li>
</ul>

<b>Network services</b> (all optional or user-triggered): OpenFreeMap and Esri map tiles, exercise images from free-exercise-db on GitHub, intervals.icu and AI assistants only if you add your own key.

Not medical advice: plans, zones and recovery scores are automatic estimates.
